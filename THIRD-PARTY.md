## Piece graphics and icons

Rasterised chess glyphs from DejaVu Sans, based on Bitstream Vera. DejaVu fonts are distributed under the permissive Bitstream Vera and public-domain modification terms. Font notice included in `vendor/DEJAVU-LICENSE.txt`.

## Positions

Original composed positions and chess move sequences from historical miniature games. Colour-reflected variants are generated by this project. Engine validation checks legality and selects a continuation; titles are teaching labels rather than detailed historical citations.
